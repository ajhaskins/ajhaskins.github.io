A.J. Haskins, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Scholar, UC San Diego

I'm a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Psychology at UC San Diego, working with Dr. Bria Long. I completed my Ph.D. in Psychological and Brain Sciences at Dartmouth College, where I worked with Dr. Caroline Robertson. My work asks what information people seek out and attend to in complex, real-world environments, using eye-tracking methods with infants, children, and adults.

I'm currently supported by an NIH D-SPAN (F99/K00) Award and a Huo Family Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship.
